About the Research Study

Research Title: Dependence on Generative AI in Academic Research: Exploring Independent Thinking and Intellectual Autonomy Among Early-Career Researchers.

Objective: To investigate how undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral researchers utilize generative AI, the extent of cognitive reliance, and whether perceived independence aligns with actual decision-making behavior.

Research Methodology

The study uses a mixed qualitative-dominant and behavioral assessment battery consisting of:

  • Baseline Dependency Assessment: Capturing self-reported usage frequencies, confidence, and task allocation.
  • Independent Problem Formulation: Evaluating unassisted hypothesis and problem identification.
  • Decision Scenarios: Testing revealed actions across 4 realistic academic crises with hidden dependency coding.
  • Content Verification: Measuring critical scrutiny against pre-formulated methodology containing planted statistical errors.
  • Metacognitive Reflection: Assessing post-task changes in perceived dependency.
